Research Interest.

My primary research interest lies in Uncertainty Quantification (UQ), with a particular focus on Conformal Prediction (CP). Conformal Prediction provides a principled framework for quantifying predictive uncertainty by constructing prediction sets with statistical coverage guarantees, rather than relying solely on point predictions. My research in this area includes:

  • Conformal Prediction for the Distribution-to-Distribution SettingAccepted at NeurIPS 2026.
  • Conformal Prediction with a Tunable Prediction Set SizeUnder review at ICLR 2026.
  • Conformal Prediction under Distribution Shift: False Coverage Proportion GuaranteesUnder review at ICLR 2026.

More recently, I have begun exploring UQ for streaming and sequential setting, with particular interests in LLM scheduling, reward hacking, and multi-agent debate.

Beyond my primary research focus, I have also contributed to research projects in Group Testing, Optimal Transport, Mixture-of-Experts Optimization, and Computer Vision.
